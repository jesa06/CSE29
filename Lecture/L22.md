# Strategies for malloc
1. first-fit allocator- navigate header by head until finds first pne big enough and puts allocation in it
    * *fast with high fragmentation*
2. best-fit allocator- navigate through all headers until we find one that exactly matches/really close to perfect fit
    * *slow with low fragmentation*
3. bump allocator
    * bump current pointer to end of slot, points to next allocation, end of one becomes the beginning of the next
    * no searching
        * *super fast but no free*
    * to free you have to go back to beginning and find out how to start bumping again
4. slot allocator
    * divide heap into fixed-size slots (64kb, really big chunky slots)
    * no use of headers to figure out status
    * just keep array, track in array which ones are used or not used (list of indices of free blocks in the heap)
    * look at list, find first free block and see if it is big enough, allocate there
    * *fast but size can be too small, update the array, more fragmentation
